Tell Claude

Claude answers here within seconds, even with your PC off. It can update statuses, viewing times and facts, answer questions about your listings, make AI summaries and write messages. If you ask it to send in your own words (“tell Sanne Saturday works”, “let Jerichostraat know I'm in”, “ask Max if Saturday works”), that counts as your OK and it sends just those, only to the places you name. Questions like “did I reply?” never send. Messages with a [YOUR ANSWER] gap wait in To do. Changes to this site itself are made in a Claude chat.
